THE CHALLENGE

Post-Meeting Action Item Decay

📋

Tasks Get Forgotten

Spoken deliverables lose owners and deadlines once calls end, causing delivery delays.

💭

Tentative Talk Clutter

Casual remarks like "maybe next week we consider X" get mistakenly logged as hard sprint tasks.

❓

Name Ambiguities

When two teammates share a name (e.g. Priya), standard automations guess and misassign cards.

🛡️

Duplicate Spam

Re-importing transcripts without cryptographic hashing spawns duplicate cards and board chaos.

AGENTIC ARCHITECTURE

5-Step Autonomous Agent Lifecycle

01 Ingest & 02 Plan

• Ingests transcript & roster with deterministic IDs.
• Regex NLP rules engine separates confirmed vs tentative items.
• Resolves owner ambiguities & executes SHA-256 deduplication.

03 Query & 04 Human Approval

• Connects to team's Trello board to inspect current lists.
• Safe Human-in-the-Loop: Proposed cards with owner, date, and transcript quotes must be approved before touching board.

05 Step 5 Independent Verification Rule

• After card creation, the agent re-reads the card from Trello REST API.
• Independently verifies that Observed Owner, Due Date, and Transcript Quote match Intended State.
• Persists verified audit records into SQLite database.
